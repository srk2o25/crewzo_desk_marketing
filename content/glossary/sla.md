## What does an SLA usually contain?

An SLA lists targets the provider commits to meet. For a help desk those are normally a first-response time and a resolution time for each priority level, plus the hours during which the clock counts.

## Why do MSPs track SLAs per client?

Each client signs its own agreement, with its own hours and priorities. Tracking one policy per client keeps the clock accurate and shows exactly which commitments are at risk. Read more in [what an MSP ticketing system is](/resources/blog/what-is-an-msp-ticketing-system/).
